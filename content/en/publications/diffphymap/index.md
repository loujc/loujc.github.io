abstract: >-
  Technology mapping at advanced nodes remains decoupled from physical design, so gate-delay objectives can identify critical paths that shift after routing and under-optimize near-critical paths. DiffPhyMap is a differentiable physical-aware technology mapper that jointly optimizes an interconnect-aware timing objective across multiple near-critical paths. Its graph-based solver is 26.4x faster than GradMap. On six IWLS'05 sequential benchmarks, DiffPhyMap reduces total negative slack by 77% and power by 13% relative to PigMap3 at the cost of 28% larger area. On 14 EPFL combinational benchmarks evaluated with ASAP7 and OpenROAD post-route timing analysis, it reduces critical-path delay by 12% on average over GradMap.
