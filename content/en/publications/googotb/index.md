abstract: >-
  Existing LLM-based verification approaches generate components through independent single-turn calls, leaving interface mismatches unresolved and reported coverage disconnected from specification requirements. GoGoTB combines an agentic execution control layer, an evolvable knowledge system, and specification-grounded coverage closure. Deterministic enforcement is separated from LLM reasoning, methodology and design-specific expertise are dispatched on demand, and every coverage bin is anchored to a named specification behavior so residual gaps can be diagnosed and addressed. Across eight RTL designs without human intervention, GoGoTB achieves 100% environment generation success and averages 98.4% line, 97.2% branch, 97.0% toggle, and 83.2% functional coverage.
