abstract: >-
  Quartet is a 22 nm digital compute-in-memory accelerator designed for diverse AI workloads. Four heterogeneous tensor engines can execute in parallel or as a pipeline, supporting static and dynamic matrix multiplication with different sparsity patterns and data formats. An off-chip-less dataflow reduces intermediate data movement across Transformers, graph convolutional networks, vision GNNs, and CNNs.
