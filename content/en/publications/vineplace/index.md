abstract: >-
  Existing analog and mixed-signal placement methods often lack explicit organization around performance-sensitive modules and leave excessive whitespace. VinePlace introduces a core-centered vine representation and hierarchical extensions for diverse AMS structures, together with geometry-level refinement and hierarchical packing for compact legal placement. Across five benchmarks ranging from a 17-device two-stage OTA to a 1,623-device SAR ADC, VinePlace reduces area by 1.03-1.52x and HPWL by 1.07-1.71x over the best-performing baselines while preserving competitive post-layout performance. The large SAR ADC results further demonstrate scalability in an automated flow.
