abstract: >-
  PowerCube is a cross-stage prediction framework for fine-grained 2D power maps. Its formula-in-the-loop architecture decouples transient physical-parameter inference from deterministic power computation, combining a spatial-signal network, a differentiable lookup-table engine, analytical equations, and layout-scale context. Evaluated with ASAP7 across eight designs, PowerCube supports layouts up to 1.5 million cells, provides a 414x average runtime speedup over commercial flows, improves average R-squared by 26.44%, and reduces normalized mean absolute error by 28.72% over the evaluated baselines.
